# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation, It hasn’t yet shown that enough users get enough value to pay and stay, so pricing can’t be the growth lever yet.

Trial-to-paid conversion is 2.0%. 128 paid customers from 6,424 trials over 13 months, flat every month (1.87%–2.08%). In a reverse trial, users have the full premium product and 98% still don’t pay. That’s a value problem, not a pricing one: most never got enough out of the premium features to miss them.

Import data usage tops out at about 45%. It peaks at 45.4% and dropped as low as 30.5%. More than half of trial users never complete the first step that makes the product useful. Without their own data there’s no forecast, and so no Aha moment.

**Model:** Freemium, - Generosity vs. reason to upgrade (the key trade-off): a richer free tier keeps users through quiet months, but fewer feel they need to pay. Free shows the problem, paid fixes it.

- Base size vs. cost to serve: more free users means a stickier base, but bank-feed connections usually cost money even for free users. Limit free to one account or less frequent syncing.

- Retention vs. clean metrics: free users cut cancellations but inflate active-user figures. Report paid retention and free-to-paid conversion separately.

- Distribution vs. revenue: free accountants and invited users bring in new trials but pay nothing directly. Measure the trials they generate.

- Simplicity vs. precision: a two-level model (free and paid) is easy to understand but has no low-cost option for occasional users. Add a middle tier only if the data shows a gap.

_____

## Recommendation

Change the paywall after the trial so it gates decisions rather than data. When the 2-week reverse trial ends, users who don’t pay keep their cash position, saved plans (as read-only plan vs. actual) and event alerts for free. What-ifs and plan editing become paid, and the upgrade prompt appears when an alert flags a cash problem.

For whom: all new trial users who reach day 14 without paying,

**The pricing bet:** f trial users keep their cash position, saved plans and alerts on the free tier, and what-ifs move behind the paywall, then trial-to-paid conversion by day 90 will rise above today’s 2%, because users stay connected until an alert shows them a problem they need to solve.

_____

## Model signal

Conversion is decided by the calendar, not by need. Value comes in burst I.e. before you hire, a tax bill etc. It fist the value creation stage, helping keep users.
