## The data pattern

This result doesn't mean importing data has no effect on conversion. Conversion is fixed at 2% of trials by formula, so a zero correlation is exactly what the formula would produce whatever users do. Testing whether importing data drives conversion needs per-user data: whether each trialist imported, and whether they paid.
